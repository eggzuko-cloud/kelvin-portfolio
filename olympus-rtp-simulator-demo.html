RTP = mean(total win)/bet over the simulated spins. The headline gauge, hit frequency, FS rate, win breakdown, run extremes and per-symbol bars are all computed at the 10–1,000 diamond bet tier, where SN-1 effectively never binds. The gold tick marks the 95% target_rtp.
Effective RTP by bet: SN-1 caps each individual spin's payout (each free spin is its own spin) at safety_net_max_win diamonds, so the cap in ×bet terms is the diamond value divided by the bet. An over-cap spin is rerolled with a fresh seed up to 3 times and the accepted roll replaces the original entirely (including its scatters and orbs); only if all rerolls also exceed the cap is the cap value credited (“pinned”). Each tier is simulated independently with full reroll semantics.
Run extremes (10–1,000 tier): Top FS multiplier = highest persistent FS total multiplier reached, shown as ×(1+value); Top base multiplier = highest summed orb multiplier on a single non-FS spin; Max cascades = most tumble steps in one spin; Top round win = largest payout from one base spin plus its entire Free Spins round, in ×bet. Bigger spin counts surface rarer extremes.
Average per occurrence (10–1,000 tier) gives the mean of those same metrics, each over the events where they apply: Avg FS multiplier = mean peak persistent multiplier per Free Spins round; Avg base multiplier = mean summed orb multiplier over base spins that had an orb-multiplied win; Avg cascades = mean tumble steps over winning base spins; Avg round win = mean payout over winning spins (RTP ÷ hit frequency, in ×bet).
Dead bonus = share of Free Spins rounds where no spin paid anything (green ≤12%, amber ≤25%, red above — competitor benchmark is single digits). In-FS hit freq = share of individual free spins that paid (symbol cluster only; scatters do not pay in FS, they only clear and can retrigger).
Large-R stress test: pools regular players (at regular bet) with a few whales (at whale bet = × regular) over many independent windows, all on the Normal table, and reports the day-to-day spread of wager-weighted RTP. Blended = everyone pooled (what a region's reported RTP looks like); Room-segmented = regular room only (whales in their own bet-range room). Volatility × is how much wider the blended RTP swings than the segmented one — the case for bet-range rooms. Whale wager share shows how a handful of high-bet users can dominate the pooled number, so their personal luck moves the whole region's short-term RTP.
σ / round = standard deviation of a single round's payout (base spin + its whole FS round) in ×bet — the game's intrinsic volatility. RTP 95% band @N = ±1.96·σ/√N, the expected spread of the RTP read at the current spin count N (halves for every 4× more spins). A wide band means you need more spins for a trustworthy RTP number, and that players feel bigger swings.
Auto-save: every config change is saved to your browser's local storage and restored automatically on reopen — but only when this HTML is opened as a downloaded local file; it does not persist inside the in-app preview sandbox. Use Download .json for portable backups. Reset to defaults clears the saved session and returns to the built-in config.
FS split (this build): each table has separate orb parameters for base game vs Free Spins — orb chance/Orb value pool drive base spins; FS orb chance/FS orb value pool drive the bonus, where orbs feed the persistent multiplier. This demo ships a balanced economy (Free Spins award 10, retrigger +5, orb values capped at 10×) where the bonus carries roughly 40–45% of the return; shifting the orb pools moves that split either way.
New-Player Welcome (NEW table): a fourth weight profile served to fresh accounts, calibrated above 100% RTP. It sits below the hard safety layers (SN-1/2/3) and above the Global RTP Governor, so a safety net still vetoes it but the governor never overrides it. Entry/exit is a standing per-account flag, not an RTP metric — personal RTP is undefined on a fresh account, so the phase is gated on new_player_spin_cap OR new_player_net_win_cap (a Diamond value pegged to the account's active bet tier), whichever comes first. Eligibility is recalculated from lifetime account statistics and the current caps on each spin; changing a cap can change eligibility. Reinstalling does not reset these statistics. The NEW profile is excluded from the journey/governor panels by design.
Win Streak Assurance: a per-player win_streak_count increments on any base-game spin (Free Spins excluded) that resolves with zero total payout, and resets on any spin that pays — forced or natural. The target is redrawn each cycle from [win_streak_min_interval, win_streak_max_interval] rather than fixed, closing the bet-timing exploit a static threshold would leave open. On reaching the drawn target — and only after the grid's full natural evaluation already resolved to zero — the game tops one symbol up to a minimum number of cells (it does not add cells on top of the natural draw, so the forced cluster lands on exactly the tier named) drawn from win_streak_payout_table (a weighted list of real Blue/Green/Yellow/Purple Gem clusters at the 8- and 10-match tiers). Every other cell resolves normally. Priority: SN-3 (force_no_win) hard-vetoes it while the counter still accumulates silently; SN-2 (force_global_cold) suppresses it like everything downstream; the Global and Personal RTP governors run unaffected; Win Streak is evaluated last. Interactions: tumbling runs completely normally after the forced cells resolve — the forced cluster pays, then refill/re-evaluate proceed from the active table, and organic follow-on wins are paid, not suppressed; the per-step multiplier-orb roll resolves unforced, deliberately, so the spin never feels scripted end-to-end; and forced spins still pass the SN-1 per-spin cap check. The feature is invisible to the player and its RTP cost is absorbed into the base weights — each card shows the measured Win Streak RTP contribution and forced-win rate (hover) so it can be Monte-Carlo signed off per table.
Dynamic table journey (governor): simulates one player spin-by-spin while the backend switches their table from their personal rolling RTP over the last rolling window spins (a proxy for the 24h window). Hysteresis: start Normal; cross above max_personal_rtp → Cold; below min_personal_rtp → Hot; return to Normal when rolling RTP crosses back through target_rtp. rtp_floor forces Hot if rolling RTP dips below it (retention guard). Setting Global RTP outside [min_global_rtp, max_global_rtp] forces every user Cold/Hot, bypassing the personal governor. Governor warm-up holds the player in Normal until N spins are in the window (prevents noise-driven transitions on tiny samples); the governor's rolling RTP is also winsorized at 50×bet per round (payouts unaffected) so a single bonus can't pin the state for a whole window. Keep journey length ≥5× the rolling window or the rolling RTP never turns over. The chart plots rolling personal RTP over time with the served table as the background band; effective RTP is the whole-journey win/bet.
Model notes: an orb is rolled at spin start and every cascade step and occupies a cell; the orb multiplier applies to the combined symbol + scatter payout (matching the server formula); FS uses a persistent multiplier capped at the FS cap and accumulates only on spins with a qualifying win; FS retrigger can fire once per free spin; rejected rerolls never update the FS persistent multiplier. Scatters never drive a cascade — only regular-symbol explosions do. New scatters may drop in on each refill and accumulate; once symbol explosions stop the total scatter count is evaluated once (pay + FS trigger), then cleared for the next round. One simplification vs server: the board is modelled as 30 independent cells (counts only, no positions), so the 6+ scatter all-columns positional condition is not enforced (treated as 10×) — this affects only the rare 6+ scatter tier.

RTP = 模拟旋转中「总赢分/投注」的均值。顶部大表盘、命中率、FS 频率、赢分拆分、运行极值和各符号柱状图，都在 10–1,000 钻石 投注档计算——该档下 SN-1 基本不触发。金色刻度标记 95% 的 target_rtp。
按投注的有效 RTP：SN-1 会把每一次单独旋转的派彩（每次免费旋转都算独立一次）限制在 safety_net_max_win 钻石以内，因此以 ×投注 计的上限 = 钻石值 ÷ 投注。超上限的旋转会用新种子重掷最多 3 次，被接受的那次完全替换原结果（含其 Scatter 与 Orb）；只有当所有重掷都仍超上限时才按上限值入账（“封顶”）。每个档位都以完整的重掷逻辑独立模拟。
运行极值（10–1,000 档）：最高 FS 倍数 = 达到的最高 FS 持续累计倍数，以 ×(1+值) 显示；最高基础倍数 = 单次非 FS 旋转上的最高 Orb 倍数之和；最大连消数 = 单次旋转内最多的连消步数；单轮最高赢分 = 一次基础旋转加其整轮免费旋转的最大派彩（以 ×投注 计）。旋转次数越多，越能显现罕见极值。
每次发生的平均值（10–1,000 档）给出上述同类指标的均值，各自在其适用事件上求平均：平均 FS 倍数 = 每轮免费旋转的持续倍数峰值均值；平均基础倍数 = 有 Orb 加成赢分的基础旋转的 Orb 倍数之和均值；平均连消数 = 中奖基础旋转的连消步数均值；平均单轮赢分 = 中奖旋转的派彩均值（RTP ÷ 命中率，以 ×投注 计）。
空奖励 = 没有任何一次旋转派彩的免费旋转轮次占比（绿 ≤12%，黄 ≤25%，超过为红 — 竞品基准为个位数）。FS 内命中率 = 有派彩的单次免费旋转占比（仅符号连线；Scatter 在 FS 内不派彩，只清除并可重触发）。
大 R 压力测试：把常规玩家（常规投注）与少数大 R（大 R 投注 = × 常规）在多个独立窗口内汇集到常规台，报告按投注加权 RTP 的逐日波动。混合 = 全体汇总（区域上报 RTP 的样子）；分房隔离 = 仅常规房（大 R 在各自的投注档房间）。波动 × 表示混合 RTP 比隔离后的摆动宽多少 — 这就是按投注档分房的理由。大 R 投注占比显示少数高投注玩家如何主导汇总数字，使其个人运气左右整个区域的短期 RTP。
σ / 轮 = 单轮派彩（一次基础旋转 + 其整轮 FS）的标准差，以 ×投注 计 — 游戏固有的波动性。RTP 95% 区间 @N = ±1.96·σ/√N，即当前旋转次数 N 下 RTP 读数的预期摆幅（旋转数每增至 4 倍，区间减半）。区间越宽，越需要更多旋转才能得到可信的 RTP，且玩家体验到的摆动越大。
自动保存：每次配置改动都会保存到浏览器本地存储，并在重新打开时自动恢复 — 但仅当以下载到本地的 HTML 文件方式打开时；在应用内预览沙盒中不会持久化。便携备份请用 下载 .json。恢复默认 会清除已保存的会话并回到内置配置。
FS 拆分（本版本）：每个台桌对基础游戏与免费旋转分别有独立的 Orb 参数 — Orb 概率/Orb 倍数池 驱动基础旋转；FS Orb 概率/FS Orb 倍数池 驱动奖励，其中 Orb 喂给持续倍数。本演示为均衡型经济（免费旋转 10 次，重触发 +5，Orb 倍数上限 10×），奖励约承载 40–45% 的回报；调整 Orb 池即可改变这一比例。
新手欢迎期（NEW 台）：面向新账号的第四套权重档位，校准至 100% RTP 以上。它位于硬安全层（SN-1/2/3）之下、全局 RTP 调控器之上：安全网仍可否决它，而调控器不会覆盖它。进入/退出由常驻的账号标记决定，而非 RTP 指标 — 新账号的个人 RTP 无定义，因此该阶段以 new_player_spin_cap 或 new_player_net_win_cap（按账号当前投注档折算的钻石值）中先达成者为准。每次旋转根据账号累计数据和当前上限重新判断资格；修改上限可能改变资格，重装不会重置累计数据。NEW 档位按设计不参与旅程/调控器面板。
连败保底：每位玩家维护 win_streak_count，在任何零派彩的基础游戏旋转（不含免费旋转）后 +1，并在任何有派彩的旋转（无论强制或自然）后清零。目标值每轮重新抽取于 [win_streak_min_interval, win_streak_max_interval]，而非固定值，从而堵住固定阈值会留下的“卡点压注”漏洞。当计数达到抽取的目标 — 且必须是棋盘完整自然结算后仍为零的那一次 — 游戏会把从 win_streak_payout_table 抽中的某一个符号补足到最少格数（不是在自然结果之上再加，因此强制组合恰好落在该表所指的档位）（该表是蓝/绿/黄/紫宝石在 8 连与 10 连档位的组合加权列表）。其余每个格子均正常结算。优先级：SN-3（force_no_win）硬否决，同时计数器静默累加；SN-2（force_global_cold）与其下游一样将其抑制；全局与个人 RTP 调控器不受影响；连败保底最后评估。交互：连消在强制格结算后完全正常运行 — 先派发被强制的组合，再按当前台桌补充并重新评估，自然衍生的后续中奖照常派发、不被抑制；每步的 Orb 掉落判定不强制，这是刻意设计，避免整次旋转显得被编排；强制旋转同样要通过 SN-1 单次上限校验。该功能对玩家不可见，其 RTP 成本被吸收进基础权重 — 每张卡片会显示实测的 连败保底 RTP 贡献与强制中奖频率（悬停查看），便于逐台做蒙特卡洛签核。
动态台桌旅程（调控器）：逐次旋转地模拟单个玩家，后端根据其在最近 滚动窗口 次旋转内的个人滚动 RTP（24 小时窗口的替身）切换台桌。滞回逻辑：从常规台开始；超过 max_personal_rtp → 冷台；低于 min_personal_rtp → 热台；当滚动 RTP 反向穿过 target_rtp 时回到常规台。若滚动 RTP 跌破 rtp_floor 则强制热台（留存保护）。把 全局 RTP 设到 [min_global_rtp, max_global_rtp] 之外会强制所有玩家切冷/热台，绕过个人调控器。调控器预热在窗口内积满 N 次旋转前把玩家保持在常规台（防止小样本上的噪声驱动切换）；调控器的滚动 RTP 还会按每轮 50×投注 做缩尾（不影响派彩），使单次奖励无法把状态钉死一整个窗口。旅程长度须 ≥ 滚动窗口的 5 倍，否则滚动 RTP 永远不会翻转。图表以时间为横轴绘制个人滚动 RTP，背景色带表示当时所在台桌；有效 RTP 为整段旅程的赢分/投注。
模型说明：Orb 在旋转开始及每个连消步各掷一次并占据一个格子；Orb 倍数作用于「符号+Scatter」的合并派彩（与服务端公式一致）；FS 使用受 FS 上限限制的持续倍数，且仅在有合格赢分的旋转上累积；FS 重触发每次免费旋转最多触发一次；被拒的重掷不会更新 FS 持续倍数。Scatter 不会驱动连消 — 只有常规符号爆开才会。每次补充时可能掉入新的 Scatter 并累积；当常规符号不再爆开时，对 Scatter 总数一次性结算（派彩 + 触发 FS），随后清除并进入下一轮。相较服务端的一处简化：棋盘被建模为 30 个独立格子（只计数量、无位置），因此 6+ Scatter 需占满所有列的位置条件未被强制（按 10× 处理）— 这仅影响罕见的 6+ Scatter 档。
